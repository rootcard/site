Who runs this home, and what it keeps.

{{home.name}} is one public home for rootcard identities. This page says who operates it, which key it signs with, what it stores and how to leave. It describes this home only, not the rootcard standard or other homes.

What this home cannot do, and how to leave

  • It cannot reset your password, recover a lost recovery card, read your vault or sign in as you. Nobody can ask the operator to do any of this: it is not technically possible.
  • Download your account file at any time from your account.
  • Move to another home, or retire the identity for good, with the recovery card and the key tool. Your id and handle stay the same when you move.

Service

  • Free to use for the {{home.realm}} realm. Usage is limited per network address and per identity; the limits are listed in this home's API.
  • rootcard is pre-1.0 and has not had an external security review. The service is offered as is, with no guarantee of availability. Keep your recovery card and an account file so you can move if this home goes away.
