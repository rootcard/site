Welcome back.

Your password unlocks your vault here in the browser, which then signs rootcard.org's one-time challenge. The password itself is never sent.

Sign in with your handle

Your username, a # and the 4-character tag from your ID card.

Forgot your password?

Nobody can reset it for you, rootcard.org included: there is no e-mail reset to hijack. Reset it yourself with your recovery card in the key tool. Your id, your handle and your data stay.

Home somewhere else?

You sign in only at your identity's home. If your home is another server, sign in there; if you want rootcard.org as your home, move with the key tool.

Public profile

Your public profile is shared with every host and service that looks you up. Once copied it cannot be taken back.

Private data

Encrypted inside your vault. rootcard.org stores it but cannot read it.

Change password

The new password replaces the one you signed in with. The change is signed with the old password, so rootcard.org can check it without ever seeing either.

At least 12 characters, not your username.

A password change stops the old password at once. After a suspected leak, reset with the recovery card in the key tool instead: that also ends every copy of the old vault.

Prove who you are

A service that wants to know "is this really you, now?" gives you a challenge and names itself as the recipient. The proof is valid only for that recipient and only while this instance is current.

A JSON statement is hashed into the challenge; say what it is for and until when, for example "I am Astra on example.social, until 2027-06-30". For a public statement, name where you publish it as the recipient.
