Key tool

The one place your recovery card goes.

Everything that changes who can access your identity, where it lives or whether it exists needs the recovery key. The key tool is the only program that asks for it: a single HTML file that you save and open from your own disk.

Download

The page is the keytool.html of the npm package rootcard@{{rootcard_version}}, unchanged. Save it, then open the saved file in your browser. It works offline, from file://, and talks only to the hosts you enter.

What it does

Reset a forgotten password

Mints a new instance at your home with a new password. Every old password, vault copy and session stops working.

Move or restore

Moves your identity to another home, carrying data, profile and passwords, or restores it after a home disappears.

Add or revoke passwords

Up to 15 passwords, each a separate lock on your vault. Revoke one, for example after losing a device.

Rotate or retire

Replace the recovery key itself, which gives you a new card, or close the identity for good.

Using it with rootcard.org

  1. Open the saved rootcard-keytool.html.
  2. Load your recovery card file (.card.json), or type the recovery key, realm global and your username.
  3. Enter rootcard.org as the host (the key tool adds https:// and checks this home's /.well-known/rootcard).
  4. Choose the operation. To move here from another home, enter rootcard.org as the target host.

Never type your recovery key into a website, this one included. A page a server sends you can be changed by that server; a file on your disk cannot.
