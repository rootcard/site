Look up an ID card

Check anyone's ID card yourself.

Type the handle of an identity homed at rootcard.org, or paste a proof someone gave you. Your browser fetches the public log and profile and verifies every signature itself: you do not have to trust this server's word.

Verify a proof

Someone sent you a proof that they control an identity, or published a signed statement. Paste it here: your browser checks it against the identity's newest log, fetched from rootcard.org when it is homed here, and stamps the result on the card.
